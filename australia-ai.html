How the index works

Take a country's share of all Claude use. Divide it by its share of the world's working-age people. 1.0 means "about what you would expect".

In September 2025 Australia was 5th at 4.1x. In the March 2026 report it was 7th. By the June 2026 report it was 1st, at more than 6x. 418

In raw traffic we are only 11th, with 1.6% of the world's use. Small country, huge per person. 1

Part 4 | Why

So why us?

"Local workforce composition is a likely factor for why adoption appears decoupled from income"

Anthropic, How Australia uses Claude 1

In plain words: it is about the kind of jobs we do, not how much we earn.

1

Lots of office jobs

NSW and VIC have more people in finance, professional services and tech. WA and NT have fewer people in the jobs where it tends to be used. The ACT has high incomes but lower use than expected, which may reflect barriers for its big public sector workforce. 1

2

Rich countries use it wider

Around the world, 1% more GDP per person goes with about 0.7% more use. Heavy-use countries use it for more kinds of tasks. In India, over half of use is coding. 4

3

Admin-heavy work

Management, office admin, work emails and business documents all show up more here than in the world data. My reading: that is the everyday work a writing helper fits best. 1

4

It is being built here

A Sydney office opened in April 2026. CommBank, Quantium, Canva and Xero are customers. ANU, Garvan, Curtin and Murdoch Children's are research partners. The federal government signed an MOU. 567

If I was your analyst, I would say:

  1. Your team already uses it.Office roles lead the numbers. So the question is how to use it well, not whether to start.
  2. Measure small wins.Most tasks are 2 to 3 hours of work. Track time saved per task, not big "AI projects".
  3. Keep a person in the loop.Australians mostly work with it, not hand off to it. Build your process the same way.
Method and sources

How I built this

I read the Anthropic Economic Index reports from September 2025 to June 2026 and the Australia report, then checked the headline numbers against Australian news coverage. All numbers are as published. I did not re-estimate any of them. Charts are hand-built in HTML, CSS and a bit of JavaScript.

Limits of this data

The data comes from privacy-safe samples of Claude.ai chats, so it does not cover all business use through the API. Reports use different time windows, so the rank change compares two published snapshots, not one smooth series. The #1 rank and "more than six times" are as reported by IT Brief and SmartCompany from the June 2026 Cadences report. State index values for QLD, SA and ACT were not published.
