The 4 traps

  • 1042 twice: the same order is in the table two times. Count both and you get 2 late orders instead of 1.
  • "delivered": lower case. To a computer, "delivered" and "Delivered" can be two different statuses.
  • "Dlvd": a short form nobody wrote down. Is it delivered? An agent has to guess.
  • Empty date on 1043: no delivery date, so nobody can say if it was late. A careful answer says so.

The right answer: 1 late order (1042), and 1 order we can't judge (1043).
